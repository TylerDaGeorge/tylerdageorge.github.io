Capabilities

Custom tooling and practical automation built around your process.

Every project begins with a specific part, process, production volume, equipment constraint, and desired outcome. Eden develops the mechanical tooling and documentation around those requirements.

Request a Project Review

Capability

Assembly and press-fit tooling

Bushing insertion, bearing installation, pin and dowel pressing, seal installation, seating, staking, alignment, controlled depth, changeable nests, orientation, and integration with an existing arbor, pneumatic, hydraulic, or servo press.

Eden can design mechanical fixtures, nests, insertion tooling, frames, guarding concepts, and system layout. Controls, electrical design, safety integration, fabrication, installation, and validation are defined separately and may require qualified project partners.

Capability

Inspection fixtures

CMM workholding, datum location, minimal-contact support, probe-access planning, multi-part fixtures, visual-inspection fixtures, checking aids, mechanical retention, and revision-controlled documentation.

The featured CMM fixture is a completed design project and the current proof point.

Capability

Production workholding

Secondary-operation fixtures, fixture plates, custom part nests, multi-part concepts, soft-jaw and custom-jaw design, quick-change concepts, modular locating, setup documentation, and build-ready drawings.

Each design is evaluated against the actual machine, part, operation, and loading needs.

Capability

Operator-assist equipment and small custom automation

Potential functions include indexed material positioning, adjustable length stops, part presentation, pressing, ejection, transfer, collection, and customer-specific cut-to-length concepts.

Eden does not currently offer a universal cut-to-length machine. Each application begins with exact material, stock form, cutting method, tolerance, burr requirement, volume, and workflow.
