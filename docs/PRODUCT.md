# 2. Company Product Direction

Eden Industrial Systems is developing **equipment for localized process environments for critical processes**.

The company should not be positioned primarily as a stainless-steel furniture manufacturer.

The broader product strategy is to create equipment that brings controlled, filtered airflow directly to the process that needs it.

Core positioning:

> **Eden builds localized environments around critical processes.**

Core customer idea:

> **Control the process environment, not the entire room.**

The goal is to give manufacturers another option when their critical process occupies a relatively small working area compared with the surrounding facility.

# 5. Product Concept

The Eden Clean Process Chamber is a compact workstation designed to create a localized HEPA-filtered work zone around a critical process.

The fundamental concept is an enclosure containing:

- An overhead filtration system
- A blower
- HEPA filtration
- An upper air-distribution plenum
- A working chamber
- A work surface or rack
- Lower exhaust openings
- An enclosure around the critical working area

Filtered air is introduced from above and moves downward through the work zone.

The goal is to concentrate environmental control around the process rather than automatically requiring the entire surrounding room to receive the same level of control.

---

# 6. Basic Airflow Concept

The current conceptual airflow path is:

**Room Air → Prefilter → Blower → HEPA Filter → Supply Plenum ↓ Work Zone ↓ Exhaust**

The intended sequence is:

## 1. Intake

Air is drawn into the filtration system from the surrounding environment.

## 2. Prefiltration

A prefilter may remove larger airborne particulate before the air reaches the primary HEPA filter.

## 3. Blower

A blower moves air through the filtration system.

## 4. HEPA Filtration

Air passes through a HEPA filter before entering the critical work zone.

## 5. Distribution

An upper supply plenum distributes filtered air across the top of the chamber.

## 6. Downward Airflow

Filtered air moves vertically downward through the working area.

## 7. Exhaust

Air exits through engineered openings near the lower portion of the enclosure.
