The staging gap

Sensitive work should not lose protection while it waits.

Components and tools are often cleaned, prepared, or inspected correctly and then left exposed while the next process step is delayed. Breaks, shift changes, inspection holds, packaging queues, and equipment availability can all create unnecessary staging time.

The CEC creates a dedicated point-of-use location for holding sensitive work closer to the process.

CEC Clean Staging Chamber

Keep sensitive work protected and close to the process.

The CEC is being developed for manufacturers and laboratories that need a compact location for staging cleaned components, tools, fixtures, samples, and work-in-process during interruptions and handoffs.

It is intended to reduce unnecessary handling, repeated bagging, and movement to remote storage areas while keeping staged items visible and accessible.
