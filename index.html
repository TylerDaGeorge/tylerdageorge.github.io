Flagship product

Meet the Controlled Environment Chamber (CEC).

The developing, self-contained benchtop enclosure is designed to create a localized controlled environment around sensitive items. Room air enters from above, passes through HEPA filtration, moves downward through the chamber, and exits through designated lower-level outlets.

Airflow concept

How It Works

The intended airflow path moves room air through filtration and distributes filtered air above the working area.

  1. 1Room air
  2. 2Prefilter
  3. 3Blower
  4. 4HEPA filter
  5. 5Supply plenum
  6. 6Work zone
  7. 7Exhaust

This airflow architecture is preliminary. Prefilter use and location, performance values, and any cleanliness classification remain subject to engineering and validation.
