# Stainless Clean Environment Table

## Product Overview

The Eden Industrial Systems Stainless Clean Environment Table is a durable, cleanable workstation intended for cleanrooms, laboratories, controlled manufacturing areas, inspection stations, assembly operations, and other environments where corrosion resistance and ease of sanitation are important.

The table uses a 16-gauge Type 304 stainless steel work surface supported by a welded stainless steel frame. The legs bolt to the welded frame for efficient shipping and straightforward field assembly. Adjustable leg levelers allow the table to be stabilized on uneven floors.

The product is available with either a solid work surface or an optional perforated work surface.

## Work Surface Options

### Solid Work Surface

The standard configuration uses a continuous 16-gauge Type 304 stainless steel work surface.

This configuration is intended for applications requiring:

* A continuous, easy-to-wipe work area
* Containment of small parts
* General assembly or inspection work
* Reduced airflow through the work surface

### Perforated Work Surface

An optional perforated work surface is available using nominal 0.25 in. diameter perforations.

This configuration may be used where airflow through the work surface is beneficial, including applications involving:

* Cleanroom airflow management
* Reduced obstruction of vertical airflow
* Integration beneath localized clean-air equipment
* Processes that benefit from particulate movement away from the work area

The final perforation pattern, spacing, open-area percentage, and edge setbacks are to be defined by engineering.

## Construction

### Work Surface

* Material: Type 304 stainless steel
* Thickness: 16 gauge
* Standard size: 30 in. deep × 72 in. wide
* Standard finished heights: 30 in. or 36 in.
* Surface configuration: solid or perforated

All exposed edges should be designed to minimize sharp corners and support safe cleaning and handling.

### Support Frame

The support frame is fabricated from Type 304 stainless steel and welded directly to the underside of the work surface.

The frame should:

* Support the work surface across its full width and depth
* Resist racking during use
* Provide repeatable mounting locations for the bolt-on legs
* Minimize unnecessary horizontal ledges and contamination traps
* Avoid exposed openings where practical

### Legs

The table legs are fabricated from Type 304 stainless steel and attach to the welded support frame using mechanical fasteners.

The bolt-on leg design allows the work surface and frame assembly to ship separately from the legs.

### Levelers

Each leg includes an adjustable leveler at its lower end.

The levelers should:

* Allow the table to be stabilized on uneven floors
* Provide sufficient adjustment range for normal facility floor variation
* Resist corrosion in the intended environment
* Include a non-marring floor contact surface when appropriate

## Assembly

The table is designed for field assembly using common hand tools.

### Assembly Sequence

1. Place the work surface and welded frame assembly upside down on a protected surface.
2. Position each stainless steel leg at its designated mounting location.
3. Install and tighten the supplied leg fasteners.
4. Turn the assembled table upright.
5. Position the table in its final location.
6. Adjust the leg levelers until the work surface is level and the table is stable.
7. Recheck all leg fasteners after leveling.

Detailed assembly instructions and torque requirements will be established before production release.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

The table should be cleaned using methods compatible with Type 304 stainless steel and the customer's controlled-environment procedures.

General recommendations:

* Use non-abrasive wipes and approved cleaning agents.
* Wipe in the direction of the stainless steel grain when a directional finish is present.
* Remove chemical residues promptly.
* Avoid carbon-steel brushes, scrapers, or tools that may contaminate the stainless steel surface.
* Inspect leg fasteners and levelers periodically.
* Replace damaged levelers or hardware as necessary.

Final cleaning-agent compatibility guidance will depend on the selected stainless steel finish, hardware, and leveler materials.
