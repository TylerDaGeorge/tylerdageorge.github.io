# CEC — HEPA-Filtered Clean Staging Chamber

## Approved position

The CEC is a compact point-of-use chamber designed to help protect cleaned components, tools, samples, trays, fixtures, and sensitive work-in-process during breaks, inspections, shift changes, handoffs, and process delays. It is a clean WIP staging and point-of-use clean storage product, not a general-purpose environmental test chamber.

The workflow problem is exposure while correctly cleaned or prepared work waits for the next operation, inspection, packaging step, operator, equipment availability, or shift. The CEC is intended to provide a protected staging area close to the operator and reduce unnecessary handling, covering, bagging, and movement to remote storage.

## Product configurations

- **Standalone CEC:** a benchtop chamber installed on a suitable existing work surface. An EIS table is not required.
- **Integrated CEW:** the CEC paired with an EIS Clean Environment Table as a complete point-of-use clean staging workstation.

## Target applications

Potential nonhazardous applications include assembly interruptions, inspection holds, breaks and shift changes, cleaned tool and fixture storage, pre-packaging staging, laboratory workflow delays, electronics and microelectronics, medical-device manufacturing, precision instruments, optical components and sensors, contract manufacturing, and quality-control or metrology staging.

## Development and pilot status

The CEC is in prototype and pilot development. Final airflow, particle-cleanliness, noise, loading, filter-service, and recovery specifications will be published only after completed product testing. No fixed price is approved; calls to action should request a pilot quote or application discussion.

## Claims restrictions

Use design-intent language such as “designed to supply HEPA-filtered airflow” and “designed to help protect sensitive work.” Do not claim a completed cleanliness class, certification, validation, sterility, guaranteed particle reduction, containment, personnel protection, or a particular flow regime. A HEPA filter does not by itself establish a chamber cleanliness classification.

## Required validation areas

Before performance claims are published, complete and document the applicable airflow, particle-cleanliness, chamber-pressure, filter-configuration and integrity, door-opening recovery, noise, loading, electrical, mechanical-stability, and serviceability testing under defined operating conditions.

## Limitations

The CEC is for contamination-sensitive, nonhazardous components, tools, samples, fixtures, and work-in-process. It is not intended for hazardous chemicals, vapor-generating processes, biological or infectious materials, cytotoxic or radioactive materials, sterile pharmacy compounding, or any application requiring environmental containment or personnel protection.

## Planned ESD option

An ESD-focused configuration is planned for electronics and microelectronics workflows. Quote intake should capture grounding, surface-resistance, material, and component-handling requirements. The standard CEC must not be described as ESD safe unless its released construction and grounding design support that claim.
