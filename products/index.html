Eden Clean Process Chamber

Localized HEPA-filtered airflow for critical processes.

The chamber is a developing localized process environment intended to create a compact work zone around sensitive operations. Filtered air is supplied from above and is intended to move vertically through the working area before exiting near the bottom of the enclosure.

Design direction

Product Features

HEPA filtration

Replaceable high-efficiency filtration is intended to provide filtered supply air.

Engineered distribution

An upper supply plenum is intended to distribute filtered air across the chamber.

Vertical airflow

Filtered air is intended to move downward through the critical work area.

Stainless construction

Durable stainless surfaces are intended to support industrial use and routine cleaning.

Clear enclosure

Transparent panels are intended to preserve visibility and define the work zone.

Serviceable design

Filters and major service components are intended to remain accessible.
