Three-Part CMM Inspection Fixture

A mechanical fixture concept developed for three delicate thin-wall PEEK components while preserving access to inspection features.

Discuss an Inspection Fixture
Design architecture
010203

Three loading stations · Spring-loaded retention · Open inspection access

Design requirements

  • Locate three parts consistently
  • Retain components mechanically
  • Preserve probe access to the flange outside diameter and upper tube inside diameter
  • Allow straightforward operator loading and release
  • Translate inspection needs into manufacturable fixture documentation

Design approach

Support, access, and release considered together.

The compact fixture uses three stations and spring-loaded mechanical retention to hold thin-wall parts while leaving inspection features accessible. This page describes a completed design project; fabrication, installation, production testing, and measured results are not claimed.
