Point-of-use clean storage for sensitive work-in-process.

Between-process protection

A cleaner process needs a cleaner place to pause.

Contamination-sensitive work does not always move directly from one operation to the next. Components may wait for inspection, packaging, another operator, available equipment, or the next production shift.

During these interruptions, work may be repeatedly bagged, covered, moved to remote storage, or left exposed near the process.

The CEC is designed to provide a dedicated staging location at the point of use.

How the concept works

HEPA-filtered airflow in a compact chamber

The CEC concept uses a top-mounted blower and replaceable filtration system to introduce HEPA-filtered air into the chamber. Air is distributed through the staging area and exits through a lower exhaust or return section.

  1. 1Room air
  2. 2Blower
  3. 3Replaceable filter
  4. 4HEPA-filtered supply
  5. 5Staging area
  6. 6Lower exhaust or return

The final plenum, diffuser, airflow rate, chamber pressure, filter configuration, and recovery performance remain subject to prototype testing.

Use limitations

Intended for nonhazardous staging applications

The CEC is being developed for contamination-sensitive, nonhazardous components, tools, samples, fixtures, and work-in-process.

It is not a fume hood, biosafety cabinet, glovebox, hazardous-material enclosure, or operator-protection device.

Not intended for

  • Hazardous chemicals
  • Solvent or vapor-generating processes
  • Biological containment
  • Infectious materials
  • Cytotoxic materials
  • Sterile pharmacy compounding
  • Radioactive materials
  • Applications requiring personnel protection
  • Applications requiring certified environmental containment

CEC FAQ

Questions about the pilot program

Is the CEC currently ISO classified?

No. Prototype performance testing is in progress. Eden Industrial Systems will publish only cleanliness, airflow, and recovery claims supported by completed testing.

Is the CEC a fume hood or biosafety cabinet?

No. The CEC is intended for nonhazardous clean staging. It is not designed to contain hazardous vapors, biological agents, infectious materials, or processes requiring operator protection.

Can the CEC be used on an existing table?

The CEC is being developed as a standalone benchtop product and as part of an integrated EIS Clean Environment Workstation. The supporting surface must meet the published size, load, stability, and service-clearance requirements.

What can be stored inside the CEC?

Potential applications include components, trays, tools, fixtures, samples, and work-in-process that fit within the final published size and loading limits.

Will an ESD version be available?

An ESD-focused configuration is planned. Customers should include their grounding, surface-resistance, material, and component-handling requirements in the quote request.

How will filters be serviced?

Final filter-access procedures, replacement intervals, and maintenance instructions will be included in the product documentation after the prototype configuration is tested.
