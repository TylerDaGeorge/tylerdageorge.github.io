# Capabilities Source of Truth

Eden provides project-specific mechanical design for assembly and press-fit tooling, inspection fixtures, production workholding, and focused operator-assist equipment. Assembly and press-fit tooling is the leading application.

Potential deliverables include 3D CAD, manufacturing and assembly drawings, bills of materials, component specifications, revision-controlled files, and vendor-ready fabrication packages.

The three-part CMM fixture is a completed design project. Fabrication, installation, production use, or measured outcomes are not established. Eden does not currently sell a standardized automatic press or universal cut-to-length machine.

Controls, electrical engineering, guarding, fabrication, installation, validation, and machine-safety responsibilities are project-specific, quoted separately, and may require qualified partners.
