Optional complete setup

Need a complete staging station?

The CSC can be supplied with an EIS stainless steel table for customers who need both the filtration chamber and a dedicated work surface.

The table is optional. The CSC can also sit on an existing workbench, laboratory bench, inspection station, assembly station, cart, or other suitable surface.
