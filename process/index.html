Engineering Process

A practical path from production problem to build-ready design.

The scope is defined around the part, current process, available equipment, and responsibilities for every project.

  1. Process review

    Review the part, workflow, volume, equipment, operator interaction, and desired outcome.

  2. Requirements definition

    Document functional needs, interfaces, tolerances, access, constraints, and project responsibilities.

  3. Concept development

    Develop a mechanical direction grounded in the actual manufacturing process.

  4. Direction approval

    Review the concept and align on scope before detailed engineering.

  5. Detailed engineering

    Resolve geometry, components, manufacturability, assembly, and documentation.

  6. Build package and support

    Deliver agreed models, drawings, specifications, and project-specific build support.

Responsibilities are defined before detail work.

Mechanical design, fabrication, controls, electrical design, guarding, installation, validation, and safety review are separate responsibilities. The proposal identifies Eden’s scope and any work requiring qualified partners.

Start with the production problem.

Share the part, current process, production volume, equipment constraints, and the result you want to improve. Eden will review the application and recommend a practical next step.
